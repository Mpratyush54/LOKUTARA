# Security and personal-data incident response

## First six hours after notice

1. Start an incident record with UTC and IST timestamps; name an incident lead.
2. Preserve relevant evidence and logs. Do not copy counselling or assessment
   content into general chat, tickets, or email.
3. Contain access safely: revoke exposed credentials/sessions, isolate affected
   paths, and preserve a restoration route.
4. Determine systems, people, data categories, geography, vendors, and ongoing
   risk. Record known facts separately from assumptions.
5. Decide whether the event is one listed in the CERT-In Directions. If it is,
   send the available initial report within six hours of noticing and follow up
   as facts develop.
6. Notify affected processors/controllers and activate contractual duties.
7. Assess personal-data notification duties under provisions in force on the
   incident date. Obtain counsel for high-risk or clinical-data incidents.

CERT-In: incident@cert-in.org.in · 1800-11-4949

## After containment

- Restore from tested clean backups and monitor for recurrence.
- Give affected people clear, factual protective steps when notification is
  required or materially reduces harm.
- Record decisions, evidence, communications, and notification timestamps.
- Complete root cause, corrective action, owner, and due date.
- Keep relevant ICT logs securely in India for the required period; restrict
  access and do not retain unrelated personal content merely because an
  incident occurred.

## Exercise

Run a tabletop exercise at least twice yearly and after a major architecture,
vendor, assessment, or counselling-data change.
