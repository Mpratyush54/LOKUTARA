# Assessment and psychometric release gate

No assessment may be described as validated, psychometric, diagnostic, or
suitable for a workplace decision until its owner records:

- construct and intended use;
- prohibited uses and consequences;
- author/publisher, licence, item-security, and administrator requirements;
- target population, language, accessibility, and Indian-context evidence;
- reliability, validity, norms, uncertainty, and adverse-impact evidence;
- scoring and report review, including misleading precision;
- participant notice, voluntariness, result audience, and withdrawal path;
- accommodations and a non-assessment alternative;
- qualified interpretation and human-review process;
- retention, export, correction, sharing, and deletion behavior;
- pilot monitoring, complaints, versioning, and revalidation trigger.

Current in-product screens are developmental conversation aids. They may not be
used alone for hiring, firing, promotion, discipline, compensation, clinical
diagnosis, treatment, disability, fitness, honesty, or risk decisions.

Before employer reporting, document participant authorisation and prefer
aggregate/de-identified reporting. Counselling records and assessment records
must remain separate.
