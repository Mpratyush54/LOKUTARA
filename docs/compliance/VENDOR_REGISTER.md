## Expected categories to verify

- Application hosting and MongoDB
- Redis/rate limiting
- Razorpay
- Email, SMS, calendar, and video services
- PostHog, Google Analytics, Microsoft Clarity, Meta, or other optional tracking
- Error monitoring, logs, backups, customer support, and file/PDF delivery

Environment variables do not constitute vendor approval. Optional analytics
must remain off before consent, and counselling/assessment content must never
be sent to those vendors.
